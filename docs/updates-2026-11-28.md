## Ticket 1 — the engine calling live collapsers safe

Ticket 1 stays **open**: `scores_validated` is still `false`, and no live-token
edge has been proven yet. What landed is the six-step repair programme aimed
at the mechanisms that were manufacturing false GREEN verdicts:

1. **GREEN-then-collapsed audit** (`6993132`) — `scripts/ticket1_green_collapse_audit.py`
   measures how many tokens scored GREEN later collapsed. Measurement only.
2. **Staleness guard** (`82f1a15`) — market snapshots older than
   `feature_market_max_age_seconds` (7200s) and forecasts older than
   `feature_forecast_max_age_seconds` (93600s) read as missing instead of
   silently scoring on dead data.
3. **Drawdown feature + tiered rule** (`0a2d5a6`) — new feature
   `drawdown_from_24h_peak`; the risk rule adds +25 points at ≥50% drawdown
   from the trailing-24h peak and +10 at ≥25%.
4. **Forecast inference + ML band floor** — forecasts now refresh between the
   24h retrains (`run_forecast_cycle` / `run_forecast_inference` in
   `forecast/engine.py`, called from the ingestion scan), and the final risk
   band is floored by the band the forecast collapse probability maps to: a
   60% collapse probability can no longer sit inside a GREEN verdict. The
   floor only raises; a missing probability applies no floor.
   Tests: `tests/test_forecast_inference.py`, `tests/test_band_floor.py`.
5. **Evidence-density label censoring** — a `collapse=0` training label must
   be earned: `forward_evidence_sufficient()` in `forecast/labels.py`
   requires at least ⌈window / 6h⌉ snapshots across the label window,
   including at least one in its final quartile. Thinly observed points are
   censored (no label written) in `LabelEngine`, in
   `seed_labels_at_feature_timestamps`, and in
   `data_lake.labels.generate_dense_labels`. `collapse=1` is never gated.
   Tests: `tests/test_label_evidence_density.py`.
6. **GREEN outcome recording** — `ScoringEngine._record_risk_outcome` no
   longer early-returns on GREEN, so GREEN verdicts enter `risk_outcomes`
   and can be evaluated like every other band — the audit in step 1 now has
   data to measure against. Tests: `tests/test_green_outcome_recording.py`.
